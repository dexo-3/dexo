Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the DEXO3 ecosystem.

What is DEXO3?

DEXO3 is a growing digital ecosystem concept that brings together education, creative resources, AI, cloud services and useful digital tools.

What does “All Simplified” mean?

It represents the goal of making digital experiences easier to discover, understand and use.

Is DEXO3 free?

Pricing and availability depend on each product. Check the relevant product page for confirmed details.

Which products are available?

Some parts of the ecosystem may be under development. A product page should clearly state whether its features are available.

Does DEXO3 have a mobile app?

An installable web app or Android app may be developed. Check the official website for current availability.

How can I contact DEXO3?

Visit the Contact page for contact options. The demonstration form currently does not send messages.

Can I suggest a feature?

You can prepare a suggestion through the Contact page once a working submission method is connected.

How do I report a problem?

Use the official contact method published by DEXO3 when one is available. Include the page address and a clear description of the issue.
